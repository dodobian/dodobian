Mauritius

Sage-Lime, woken up by the tropics: jungle green, lagoon turquoise, flamboyant red. Yellow stays only in the sunset. The dodo lived only on Mauritius, so here it is at home.

Palette

Nine colours of the island

The bright colours go to artwork and the character. The interface gets calm bases and two accents: lagoon for actions, lime for focus and indicators.

Interface

Light and dark

Classic in behaviour: an Applications / Places / System menu, buttons in the title bar, a window list and workspaces at the bottom. Modern in look: rounded windows and soft shadows, no glass.

Character

The dodo with a mouse

  • SilhouetteRound body, big head, large hooked beak. Reads even at 16 pixels.
  • ColourSage-grey feathers, a cream tuft on the tail, a greenish-yellow beak with a dark tip, like the real dodo's.
  • SignatureA corded mouse, like a pet on a leash. A joke on the "classic, mouse first" principle.
  • DetailsA flamboyant flower behind the ear. Depending on the scene: a coconut, a hammock, a pirogue, a straw hat.
  • AvoidPoses and props of other projects' mascots: our dodo stays its own character.

Draft prompt for an image generator

Flat vector storybook illustration with soft painterly shading. A round, friendly cartoon dodo bird mascot: sage-grey feathers, cream curly tail tuft, green-yellow hooked beak with a dark tip, big glossy eyes, a red flamboyant flower tucked behind its head. It walks a small cream computer mouse on its cable like a pet.
Scene: Mauritius at golden hour. Le Morne Brabant above a turquoise lagoon, a white reef line, coconut palms, a red flamboyant tree, green jungle hills, a small pirogue boat.
Palette: jungle green and lagoon turquoise dominant, small mango-gold accents only in the sun. No yellow dominance.
16:9 desktop wallpaper, calm empty water in the centre for windows.
