Dodobian logo

Marks from the GPT drawing, traced to vector. The hibiscus is recoloured to flamboyant red, and every colour is snapped to the "Mauritius" palette. There are three marks, each for its own range of sizes, plus the brand swirl.

Main logo

Emblem

A dodo head with a hibiscus. The white sticker outline keeps the silhouette on dark panels. For 48 pixels and up: login screen, About, splash, website.

Wordmark

The D mark as the first letter

The "D" mark sits on the baseline and matches the cap height. Then comes "odobian". The typeface is Nunito ExtraBold for now, as a draft: the final wordmark is converted to outlines.
